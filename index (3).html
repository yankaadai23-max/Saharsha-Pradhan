On the court

Basketball is where I learn discipline, patience and how to keep going when the game gets hard. These are the parts of my game I work on every week.

  • Shooting

    Repetition is everything. I work on form and consistency from every spot on the floor.

  • Ball handling

    Tight control with both hands, so I can read the defense instead of watching the ball.

  • Defense

    Footwork, effort and communication. Good defense starts with wanting it.

  • Teamwork

    The best possessions are the ones where everyone touches the ball.

  • Conditioning

    Strength, speed and stamina, so I can play at full pace until the final whistle.

Hard work shows up on the scoreboard.
